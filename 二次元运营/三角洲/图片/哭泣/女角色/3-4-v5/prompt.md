# 3-4-v5：完全无哭泣痕迹的悲愤版

## 目标

以 `3-4-v4` 为身份、构图和画风基准，重新生成 5 个角色。彻底移除一切“正在哭”或“刚哭过”的视觉信号；悲愤只通过眉眼肌肉、凝视、下颌、嘴唇和手部张力表达。

## 通用 Prompt

```text
Use case: identity-preserve
Asset type: 3:4 vertical female game-character portrait for an existing image set

Input image:
- Image 1 is the authoritative reference and edit target for identity, adult facial structure, hairstyle, costume, equipment, pose, hands, composition, lighting, palette and painterly semi-realistic style.

Primary request:
Regenerate the portrait with absolutely no visual evidence of crying, tears, or having recently cried. The face and eyes must be completely dry and clean.

Remove all crying cues:
- no tear streams, tear tracks, tear beads, hanging droplets, pooled tears or tear-like highlights;
- no watery, glossy or glassy eyes;
- no wet lower-lid rim or enlarged tear meniscus;
- no red, pink, irritated, bloodshot or swollen eye rims;
- no puffy eyelids, damp eyelashes, wet cheeks, flushed crying nose, smeared makeup or damp skin;
- no sobbing mouth, trembling lip or defeated crying posture.

Required dry-eyed expression:
Keep a powerful grief-and-indignation emotion without crying: sharply knitted brows, deep vertical glabella tension, slightly lowered brow ridge, narrowed focused eyes, steady confrontational eye contact, restrained hurt, injustice, accusation and defiance. Keep the jaw set, lips firmly compressed and hands tense. The emotion should read as someone forcing the pain inward and confronting the source of injustice, not as sadness alone and not as rage alone.

Eye appearance:
Natural clear sclera, normal dry eyelid rims, crisp irises and controlled catchlights. No moisture emphasis and no tear-like reflections. Preserve the character's original eye color.

Invariants:
Preserve character identity, mature adult facial proportions, hairstyle, headwear, mask if present, costume, tactical equipment, hand anatomy and placement, pose, crop, camera angle, background, lighting, contrast, palette, texture and exact 3:4 composition. Do not soften the brow tension or emotional impact.

Avoid:
No crying cues of any kind, no red eyes, no wet eyes, no tears, no wet skin, no sad puppy eyes, no helpless expression, no screaming, no rage-only expression, no smile, no face redesign, no baby face, no giant irises, no beauty-filter skin, no extra objects, no text, no logo, no watermark, no signature.
```

Preserve Butterfly's corrected mature adult face: elongated adult oval face, defined cheekbones and jaw, structured nose, narrower almond-shaped eyes and a smaller eye-to-iris ratio. Do not restore a round baby face, giant irises, anime-doll or shoujo proportions.
